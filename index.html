About

I build benchmarks and run experiments on robot foundation models, and I study the brain as a learning system. I'm currently working on SO-101 Bench, a real2sim2real benchmark for the low-cost SO-101 arm, and the Atlas of World Modeling in the Brain. Before going independent I was a research scientist at Southwest Research Institute. I hold an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence and a B.S. in Computer Science from UT San Antonio.

Projects

SO-101 Bench: Accessible Spatial Manipulation 2025 – present

A real2sim2real benchmark for evaluating robot foundation models on the SO-101 arm. 8,000 teleoperated demos, an Isaac Lab digital twin with sim2real transfer from sim data only, and VLAs, WAMs, and agents fine-tuned and evaluated under a matched compute budget. CoRL 2026 Late Breaking Results; poster at a CVPR 2026 workshop. Code · Data

Atlas of World Modeling in the Brain 2025 – present

A computational atlas of the brain as a learning system: an ML-style account of its architecture, learning rules ("optimizers" such as cortex and cerebellum), training data, and developmental curriculum.
